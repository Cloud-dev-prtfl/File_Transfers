The "Lead Detective" Enricher

Function: Takes a new Lead record (company name & website) and uses AI to browse the web, finding their Annual Revenue, CEO Name, and Tech Stack, then populates those fields in NetSuite automatically.

Use Case: "Enrich the new leads from today's trade show."


SEO Product Description Generator

Function: Reads the dry technical specs of an Item (e.g., "Cotton, Size M, Blue") and generates a creative, SEO-optimized product description for SuiteCommerce or Shopify.

Use Case: "Write catchy web descriptions for the new Spring Collection items."


Intelligent Inventory Rebalancer

Function: Analyzes stock levels across multiple locations (e.g., Warehouse A vs. Warehouse B) against local demand trends. It suggests Transfer Orders to move stock where it sells fastest.

Use Case: "Analyze our West Coast inventory and suggest transfers to balance stock."


Automated "Customer Apology" Logic
Link ChatGPT to your Case Management and Credit Memo records. If a customer case is flagged with "High Sentiment Anger," the tool can automatically draft a personalized apology email and propose a credit memo amount based on the customer's Lifetime Value (LTV).

Uniqueness: It combines emotional intelligence (sentiment) with financial authority (LTV-based discounting).

Standard Gap: Standard workflows are "if/then" based; this tool uses "nuanced reasoning."



Market-Aware Price Optimizer
This tool connects NetSuite’s Item Records to ChatGPT’s browsing capability (or a pricing API). It can automatically suggest price adjustments based on real-time competitor data or inflation indices.

Uniqueness: It brings "external world" context into your ERP. Standard NetSuite forecasting (IPM) is mostly historical and internal.

Use Case: "Hey ChatGPT, check the current market price for [Item X] and suggest a discount for my top 10 customers that keeps our margin above 20%."
