Project Realities

Key assumptions and constraints that will guide the development process.

Assumptions

  • All payment processing will be handled by a secure, third-party gateway (e.g., Stripe, PayPal).
  • The platform will initially support major currencies like USD, EUR, and GBP.
  • Sellers are responsible for the accuracy of their listings and timely fulfillment of orders.

Constraints

  • The initial MVP will not support complex international tax and customs calculations.
  • The project has a firm six-month deadline for the MVP launch.
  • There is a defined budget for third-party services like payment gateways and CDNs.
